3. Stay safe around running vehicles

  • Never run an engine in a closed garage or other enclosed space: exhaust contains carbon monoxide, which can kill. Record outdoors or with the garage door fully open.
  • Keep hands, hair, clothing, cables and the phone clear of fans, belts and pulleys; electric fans can start without warning.
  • Don’t touch hot exhaust, manifolds or the radiator, and don’t hold the phone in the exhaust stream.
  • Make sure the vehicle is in park (or neutral with the parking brake on), and stay out of its path. Watch for traffic.
  • Only record vehicles you own or have permission to record.
